# Contributing (from the Group 3 contract)

## Branches and PRs
- No direct pushes to `main`. Create a branch first: `feature/<name>`, `fix/<name>` or `chore/<name>`.
- Every PR has a short description: what changed and why.
- Major PR: a review from every member before merging. Small PR: one review is enough.
- Reviewers respond within 48 hours, all days.

## Definition of Done
- Implemented per the agreed requirements and acceptance criteria
- Tested by at least one other team member
- Documented where applicable
- Integrated into the project (not just on a local machine)
- No critical errors or blockers remain

## Tools
Jira for tasks, GitHub for code, Discord/WhatsApp for communication.
